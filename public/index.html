What we make

Sounds shouldn't always come off the shelf.

Haute Sound Couture specialises in voices, vocal samples and heavily processed human sound — from DJ tags, spoken hooks and transition effects to cinematic details, atmospheres, textures, experimental sounds, podcast tools and custom collections that do not fit neatly into a useful category.

Some are ready to use. Some are made for a specific purpose. Some don't exist until someone asks for them. The point is not to fill a folder with more generic material. It is to make the useful strange things: sounds with a clear job, a human origin and enough character to belong to one project instead of everybody's library.

Sound is where this started. The same instinct — human taste doing what AI alone can't — is the whole method, not just the sound method. Expect it to show up elsewhere from this maison, in time.
