Refund Policy.

Last updated 23 September 2026

Not happy with a purchase? Ask for a refund within 14 days and you get your money back in full.

How to ask

Our orders are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record, who handles all refunds. You can:

  • find your order at paddle.net (using the email on your receipt) and request a refund there, or
  • email us at contact@hautesoundcouture.com with your order number, and we'll arrange it with Paddle.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

What's covered

  • Sample Atelier and The Edit: refundable within 14 days of purchase, for any reason, including after you've downloaded the files.
  • Failed delivery: if your files can't be produced or delivered, we refund you in full automatically — you don't need to ask.

After a refund

A refunded purchase no longer carries a licence, so please stop using and delete the files from that order.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to — and never replaces — any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
